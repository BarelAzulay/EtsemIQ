# Etsem.IQ — full review, October 2026

A whole-site audit run before the October quality upgrade. I loaded the page in
Chromium (desktop and 375px) and clicked through every screen in all five
languages. I also checked the i18n key coverage with a script and read the code.
Line numbers refer to `index.html` as it stood on `main` at `4d37096`.

Every item below is marked **Fix** (done in this upgrade), **Owner** (needs a
decision or action outside the code) or **Leave** (fine; don't churn it).

---

## Critical

| # | Finding | Where | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Every signup gets PLUS for free.** `doSignup()` writes `plus:true`. | 3606 | **Fix.** Signup creates a free account. PLUS comes only from a verified Whop membership. |
| C2 | **The 6-month and annual plans use a simulated card form.** It grants PLUS for any card number that passes the Luhn check, and no money is taken. | 3949–3997 | **Fix.** These plans are hidden until they exist on Whop. Monthly is the only plan. |
| C3 | **Real Whop payers never get PLUS.** Checkout redirects to Whop, and nothing on the way back recognizes the membership. | 3949 | **Fix.** "Sign in with Whop" goes through a new Cloudflare Worker (`api/`), which checks the membership server-side. |
| C4 | **The monthly price is wrong on screen.** It shows "$19.99 today", but the Whop link applies the `FIRSTMONTH199` promo, so the charge is $1.99. | 3920 | **Fix.** The screen now shows "$1.99 first month, then $19.99/month". The checkout URL is unchanged. |
| C5 | **"Cancel" only flips localStorage.** The copy promises "no further charges", but Whop keeps billing. | 6605, `set.canceldone` | **Fix.** Cancel now sends members to Whop's subscription management page. The copy is rewritten. |
| C6 | **Admin access** cannot be made secure without a server. | — | **Fix.** Admin is decided only by the Worker: your Whop user ID (a secret) or the company admin role. Typing an email unlocks nothing. |

## Medium

- **Logout leaks the previous user's data.** The report, game progress and bests carry over to the next person. **Fix.**
- **Switching language mid-question unlocks the answer.** **Fix.**
- **Self-XSS.** Bob's chat and the user's name are written with `innerHTML`. **Fix.**
- **The analysing timeout and the urgency interval keep running after you leave the screen.** **Fix.**
- **Validation errors are hard-coded in English** (signup, login, card form). **Fix**, translated into all 5 languages.
- **"~22 min" renders as "min 22~" in RTL.** **Fix.**
- **The email-gate stat reads "99% people took the test today"** because it uses the wrong caption key. **Fix** (the caption only).
- **Games start with the 30-item free-test line** ("Thirty items, easy to extreme"). **Fix.** They use the `levelStart` pool.
- **Quiz games need an extra "Next" tap after every answer.** **Fix.** They auto-advance.
- **There is no low-time warning.** `.timer.low` is styled but never applied. **Fix.**

## Accessibility

**Fix:**
- Plan cards, the logo, the nav links, and the Memory Matrix and Logic Quest tiles are clickable `div`s and `a`s without an `href`. They become real buttons.
- Every remaining input has a label: 10 of 10. The simulated card and billing forms were deleted outright.
- Icon buttons get accessible names.
- Dialogs and the mobile sheet trap focus.
- `aria-live` is added to game status lines and Bob's chat.
- Low-contrast white-on-yellow tile numbers are fixed.
- Colour-only rules get a shape cue.

**Leave:** `lang`/`dir` switching, and the dialog's initial focus. Both work correctly.

## Performance

The page is 587 KB raw and 187 KB gzipped. DOMContentLoaded fires in about 110 ms. Boot is fine.

**Fix:**
- 29 infinite animations, including five large blurred blobs, keep running in background tabs. They now pause while the tab is hidden, and the blur is reduced on phones.
- The ticker and other intervals stop when hidden.
- Add `preconnect` for fonts, a favicon and a meta description.

**Leave:**
- The single-file architecture.
- The size of Bob's knowledge tables. They gzip well, and splitting them would add a request for little gain.

## Code health

**Fix:**
- Remove `screen-hardcore` from `ACTIVITY_FLOW`.
- Keep the no-op `navTo`. The mobile Games button still calls it, and it costs nothing.
- Wire up `reloadBests`, which is defined but never called.
- Fix stale comments ("40 levels").
- Fix the "even" repair that maps odd numbers to odd numbers.
- Fix "Out of moves" being followed by "LEVEL PASSED".

**Leave:** about 20 unused i18n keys. They are harmless and cheap.

## Trust and marketing elements — owner's decision: **Leave**

The owner chose to keep these as they are:
- the scrolling "just scored" ticker;
- the static "6,204 people took the test today" and "128 average" stats;
- the 4.8★ / 1,842 rating and the "Verified test-taker" testimonials;
- the 10-minute "discounted first month" countdown;
- the certificate's "Official result" / "Dr. A. Voss" / `etsemiq.com/verify`.

They are recorded here because consumer-protection rules (the FTC rule on fake reviews, EU unfair-commercial-practice rules) and Whop's marketplace guidelines can apply to them. Revisit them before any paid advertising.

**Exception:** the cancellation copy (C5) is not marketing. It is a false statement about billing, so it is fixed.

## Leave alone (works, don't churn)

- The i18n structure (complete in all 5 languages) and the `T_EXT` merge.
- The exit guard centralised in `showScreen`.
- Bob's matching engine and its honest fallback.
- The music engine (off by default, started only on a gesture).
- The mobile layout (no overflow on any of the 21 screens).
- Hardcore's shuffling and its per-item review.
- The Speed and Spatial generators (no duplicate options in 1,200 samples).
- The scored test not revealing right or wrong answers.
- The honest "training transfer" copy and the footer disclaimer.

## Outcome (end of the October upgrade)

Every **Fix** above shipped on `claude/quality-upgrade`. A few things changed
along the way:

- **Leave, after all:** `navTo` stays (see above).
- **Found later and fixed:**
  - Logging out now also ends the Whop session.
  - A language switch in the middle of Memory Matrix, Logic Quest or the
    memorise screen now updates the text.
  - The estimated range is clamped to 68–148.
  - Bob's floating button no longer covers answers on phones.
  - The 3 px nav overflow in Spanish (and 15 px in Russian) at 320 px is gone.
- **Independent checks:**
  - **Free-test items:** all 61 forms were solved blind. There were no wrong
    keys and no ambiguous items. Two legibility fixes followed.
  - **Trivia facts:** all 116 keys are correct. 10 explanations were made more
    precise and 3 Word Wit items were tightened.
  - **Auth security review:** nothing could be forged. Three medium items were
    hardened.
- **Size:** the page grew from 187 KB to 415 KB gzipped, mostly from the
  trivia and word banks and the parallel test forms in five languages.
  DOMContentLoaded went from about 255 ms to 287 ms on desktop, and from about
  600 ms to 690 ms on a 4×-throttled phone. A follow-up could move Bob's
  knowledge base and the trivia bank into a lazily fetched JSON file.
